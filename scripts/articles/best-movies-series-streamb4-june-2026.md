**How often is new content added?**
STREAMB4 adds new titles weekly. The exact cadence varies by content type — theatrical releases typically arrive 4–12 weeks after cinema debut, TV series are added within days of their streaming release windows.

Not currently. STREAMB4 is a streaming service — content plays over your internet connection. Offline downloading is not supported by IPTV player apps in the same way as dedicated streaming apps.

No — they're separate. Live channels play in real-time like broadcast TV. The VOD library lets you start any title at any time, pause, rewind, and resume. Both are included in your STREAMB4 subscription.
