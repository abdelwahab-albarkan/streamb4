## Samsung Smart TV Installation (Tizen OS) {#samsung}

Samsung's Tizen OS does not support sideloading. You must use an app from the Samsung App Store.

**Recommended apps:** Smart IPTV, IPTV Smarters, SS IPTV

**Steps:**
1. Press the Home button on your remote
2. Navigate to the App Store (magnifying glass icon)
3. Search for "Smart IPTV" or "IPTV Smarters"
4. Install the app and open it
5. Select "Add playlist via URL" and enter your STREAMB4 M3U URL
6. Add your EPG URL for the TV guide
7. Press Save — your channels load automatically

## LG Smart TV Installation (webOS) {#lg}

LG's webOS supports IPTV apps through the LG Content Store.

**Recommended apps:** Net IPTV, IPTV Smarters, TiviMate (available on some webOS versions)

**Steps:**
1. Press the Home button and select the LG Content Store
2. Search for "Net IPTV" or "IPTV Smarters"
3. Install and open the app
4. Choose "M3U URL" setup and paste your STREAMB4 link
5. Add your EPG URL when prompted
6. Save and your full channel list appears

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

**Channels load but video is blank**
Likely a codec compatibility issue. Switch to a different player app. TiviMate and XCIPTV support the widest range of stream formats.

**Buffering during 4K content**
Use a wired ethernet connection. Smart TVs often have a LAN port — connect directly to your router for stable 4K throughput. If ethernet isn't possible, position your router within 5 metres of the TV.

**EPG (TV guide) not showing times correctly**
Ensure your TV's time zone is set correctly in Settings. Refresh the EPG manually in your IPTV player after changing time zones.

**Channels loading slowly (20+ seconds)**
Large playlists slow down some older apps. Try XCIPTV, which uses lazy loading to display channels faster on TVs with limited RAM.

## FAQ {#faq}

**Will STREAMB4 work on my TV if it's 5 years old?**
As long as your TV runs Android TV, webOS, or Tizen OS (Samsung 2016+), STREAMB4 works through a compatible player app. Very old Smart TVs (pre-2016) may not have a compatible IPTV player in their app store — in that case, attach an Amazon Firestick or Android TV box.

**Do I need a separate subscription for each TV?**
No. A single STREAMB4 Duo, Family, or higher plan covers multiple TVs under one account. All screens must be used by the same household.

**Does STREAMB4 include the TV guide (EPG)?**
Yes. STREAMB4 provides an EPG URL with all plans. Enter it in your IPTV player's EPG settings to see programme schedules, descriptions, and catch-up indicators.

**What internet speed does my Smart TV need?**
10 Mbps minimum for HD. 25 Mbps for stable 4K Ultra HD. For multiple TVs streaming simultaneously, use 25 Mbps per 4K stream as a baseline.
