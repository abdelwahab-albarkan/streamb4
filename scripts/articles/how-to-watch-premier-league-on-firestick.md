## Step-by-Step Setup on Firestick {#setup}

### Step 1 — Enable Unknown Sources
Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → Apps from Unknown Sources → toggle ON.

### Step 2 — Install the Downloader App
Search "Downloader" on your Firestick and install the free AFTVnews app. This lets you sideload any APK.

### Step 3 — Install TiviMate
Open Downloader and enter the TiviMate APK URL (available from the TiviMate official website), or use our [Devices & Downloader Codes page](https://streamb4.com/devices) for the 6-digit shortcode. Install and open TiviMate.

### Step 4 — Add Your STREAMB4 Playlist
In TiviMate: Add Playlist → M3U URL → paste your STREAMB4 M3U link from your welcome email → EPG URL (also in your welcome email) → Save. Your channels load automatically.

### Step 5 — Find Premier League Channels
Use TiviMate's search or browse the Sports category. Look for Sky Sports Main Event, Sky Sports Premier League, TNT Sports 1, or the dedicated match streams that appear on matchday.

## Common Problems & Fixes {#troubleshooting}

**Channels not loading after entering credentials**
Double-check your M3U URL and EPG URL from your welcome email — copy/paste rather than typing manually. If the issue continues, contact STREAMB4 support via live chat (response under 5 minutes).

**Buffering during matches**
Check your internet speed first. A wired connection is strongly recommended for live sports. If speed is adequate, try switching to a different channel stream — STREAMB4 often provides multiple server mirrors per channel.

**Black screen with sound**
This usually indicates a codec issue with your Firestick's video decoder. In TiviMate settings, change the video decoder from Auto to Software. Alternatively, switch to the XCIPTV player which handles a wider range of stream formats.

**Missing match on kick-off day**
Some Premier League streams go live 30 minutes before kick-off rather than 24 hours in advance. Refresh your channel list within TiviMate if a match channel isn't showing yet.
