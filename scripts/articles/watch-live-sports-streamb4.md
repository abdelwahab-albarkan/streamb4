**Using the EPG (TV Guide)**
Your STREAMB4 subscription includes full EPG data. In TiviMate or IPTV Smarters, switch to the EPG view to see what's on now and upcoming. You can set reminders for specific matches.

Some events get dedicated match-day streams named after the fixture. These appear in the sports category a few hours before kick-off.
