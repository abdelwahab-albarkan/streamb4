## How the Reseller Program Works {#how-it-works}

The model is simple:

1. You purchase a block of IPTV connections from STREAMB4 at wholesale prices
2. You sell those connections to end customers at your chosen retail price
3. You keep 100% of the margin between wholesale and retail
4. Customers connect to STREAMB4's servers — you provide the credentials through your reseller panel
5. STREAMB4 handles all infrastructure, maintenance, server uptime, and content

You are the merchant. STREAMB4 is the silent infrastructure provider. Your customers never know STREAMB4 exists — this is a fully white-label operation.

This isn't affiliate marketing (where you earn a percentage). As a reseller, you own the customer relationship, set your own prices, and keep all profit above your wholesale cost. ## FAQ {#faq}

**Is there a minimum monthly commitment?**
No. Credits do not expire. If you don't sell for a month, your unused credits carry over. There is no monthly fee or activity requirement.

**Can I become a reseller if I have no technical background?**
Yes. The technical work is handled entirely by STREAMB4. Your role is customer acquisition and account management through the panel — both require no technical knowledge beyond basic internet use.

**Can I create sub-resellers?**
Enterprise-tier resellers can create sub-reseller accounts, building a distribution network beneath them. This is typically for resellers serving multiple cities or regions.

**What support does STREAMB4 provide for reseller customers?**
STREAMB4 provides technical support to you as the reseller. For end customer issues, you are the first point of contact — if the issue requires infrastructure-level investigation, you escalate to STREAMB4's support team. Most setup issues can be resolved with the guides on this blog without escalation.

**How do I handle refunds for my customers?**
Refund policy is at your discretion as the reseller — you set your own terms. STREAMB4's retail refund policy does not apply to wholesale reseller accounts. Many resellers offer a 24-hour test period before full payment.
